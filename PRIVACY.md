## Information used and why

Enquiries and orders can include your name or business contact details, email address, messages, agreed scope, delivery dates and payment or refund references. These are used to answer enquiries, confirm the work, deliver and support the service, arrange payment or refunds, resolve disputes and keep necessary transaction records. Please provide only what is needed. Without a way to reply or sufficient scope information, a quote or delivery may not be possible.

After scope and a private transfer channel are agreed, the service accepts one non-sensitive operational CSV and its generic field rules. The file is used to produce the agreed report and included recheck. A checksum, file size, counts and issue positions identify and explain the work. Remove unrelated personal information from both data and field names. Do not send customer/order exports, payroll, health information, card details, passwords, API tokens or other secrets.

## Correspondence, hosting and AI assistance

Email correspondence is held and handled through Google Gmail. Relevant enquiry, order and support messages, including the contact details they contain, can be read and processed by the authorised AI assistant through OpenAI/Codex to handle the service and prepare replies. Keep unnecessary personal or confidential information out of messages. This correspondence handling is separate from the local processing of your CSV.

The public demonstration is hosted on GitHub. GitHub handles visitor and account information under its own policies. Public issues and comments are not a private intake channel; do not post files or personal information there. Customer files and correspondence are not published in the demonstration repository.

The raw CSV is processed in access-restricted local working storage and is never submitted to external AI tools. AI-assisted interpretation uses source-value-free findings and the agreed generic rules. If the agreed transfer channel is a cloud or order platform, that provider can still hold the transferred file under its own terms; local checking does not mean that the transfer was cloud-free. The channel and its handling arrangements are agreed before intake.

## Payments

If Stripe is activated and used for your order, enter payment information only in its payment flow, not in email, public comments or the CSV. Stripe processes that information; the service uses the transaction reference, payment status and necessary billing/contact information to administer the order, refunds and records. This notice does not itself mean Stripe checkout is available. Any different payment channel must be agreed before payment.

Provider handling depends on the service and account settings in use. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and applicable service terms, and [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/au/privacy). Provider retention and international processing are separate from the local file arrangements below. No particular country of provider storage or processing is promised.

## Retention and requests

The local file retention date is agreed before intake, normally seven days after the included recheck window closes. Local inputs, working reports, notes and any approved backups are removed at the agreed date, with completion recorded. Deletion is a manual task, not an automatic timer.

Essential correspondence, scope, invoice, payment/refund and dispute records may be retained longer to complete obligations, resolve issues and meet record-keeping requirements. These records should not retain unnecessary CSV contents. Copies, logs and backups held by email, AI, payment, transfer and hosting providers follow those providers' terms and settings; local deletion is not a promise that every provider copy has been deleted.

Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion, or to raise a concern. Requests will be reviewed against the existing order information. If information must be retained or a provider-held copy cannot be removed through this service, the response will explain that limitation. A request will not be treated as permission to disclose your information publicly.
